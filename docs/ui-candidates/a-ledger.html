A · Ledger

The day as a page in a well-kept paper ledger: times in the margin, entries in ink, blanks left as dotted lines waiting to be filled in.

  1. Margin carries time. Start times sit left of a double red margin rule; the entry and its duration read across like an account line.
  2. Ink, not chips. Tags are small-caps words in a muted ink with a pen underline. Unlabeled blocks are a dotted blank line — visibly "to be filled in".
  3. One seal, one action. Mark is a round ink stamp at thumb reach; capture feedback is the same stamp pressing down.
  4. Serif for numbers, grotesk for controls. Newsreader tabular figures for the record; Inter only for labels and buttons.
  5. Paper by day, ink-navy by night. Same ruling and margin in both modes; inks are re-mixed per mode to stay AA on their paper.
