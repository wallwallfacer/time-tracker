Time Tracker · UI candidate

D · Calm

Soft One UI cards on a warm canvas. The day reads like a stack of pastel notes, and Mark sits under your thumb like a camera shutter, which fits the “side key replaces camera” idea.

  • Look high, tap low. The day title fills the top third. Day controls, cards and Mark sit in the lower, thumb-reachable two thirds, as in One UI.
  • A tag colors the whole card. A labeled block tints its card with a pastel of the tag hue, and the text uses a dark shade of the same hue (WCAG AA).
  • Unlabeled blocks look unfinished. White cards with a dashed outline and a soft “Tap to label” hint ask for attention without nagging.
  • Only the current block moves. It has a slow breathing glow and a shimmer line. Everything else holds still.
  • Warm in the dark too. Dark mode uses charcoal (#1E1B19), not pure black, with muted pastels, so evening labeling feels gentle.
