Week & Month

Three ways to zoom out from the Day rail, drawn in the shipped Rail system. Each candidate shows Week (light and dark) and Month. Same generated sample data in all of them: October 2026, now is Mon Oct 26 14:44, and today matches the Day screen's sample exactly.

flag
This crosses an MVP non-goal. The spec lists "statistics, charts, summaries" as out of scope, and the Rail brief says "Layout, not a chart". Whichever candidate wins, the spec's non-goals need an update.

Shared by all three

  • Day · Week · Month segmented button under the app bar. ‹ › steps one period, tapping the title jumps back to the current one.
  • Weeks start Monday. Blocks that cross midnight are split per day, like the Day screen already does.
  • Tap any day (column, card or cell) to open that day in Day view.
  • Unlabeled time keeps the Day screen's hatch, so "not done yet" reads the same everywhere.
  • No Mark button on Week/Month: the hardware double-press still marks; the overview is for reading.

Worth deciding alongside the pick

  • Sleep is the biggest tag every day. Show it, or let it be hidden from totals?
  • Mark button on overviews: hide (as drawn) or keep?
  • Mix and match: e.g. A's rhythm grid with B's totals list below it.
